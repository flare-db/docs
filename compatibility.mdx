title: "Compatibility Matrix"
---

This matrix clarifies the capabilities of FlareDB. Individual capabilities are grouped by their corresponding **What / Where / When / How** question:

- **What** results are being calculated?
- **Where** in event time?
- **When** in processing time?
- **How** do refinements of results relate?

For more details on the What / Where / When / How breakdown, see the [Streaming 102](https://www.oreilly.com/radar/the-world-beyond-batch-streaming-102/) post on O'Reilly.

## How to read the tables

Each row is a property. Cells answer: _does FlareDB have this property?_

| Sign | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ✅ | Yes |
| 🟡 | Partially |
| 🗓️ | Planned |
